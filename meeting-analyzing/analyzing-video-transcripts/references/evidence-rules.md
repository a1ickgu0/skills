# Evidence and status rules

Use these literal labels in the report:

- `speech-only`: stated in the transcript, without independent corroboration.
- `slide-only`: visible in supplied OCR or slide evidence, but not stated in speech.
- `demo`: presented as a live or recorded demonstration.
- `customer-claim`: attributed to a customer or customer example.
- `official-cross-check`: checked against a supplied authoritative source.
- `analysis-inference`: analyst interpretation, not a source statement.
- `uncertain`: incomplete, ambiguous, conflicting, or low-confidence evidence.
- `future-looking`: aspiration, roadmap, prediction, or future commitment.

Attach at least one label to each important claim. Multiple labels may apply. Include timestamps for transcript-derived evidence and identify the external source for cross-checks.

Numbers require speaker/source attribution, unit, timeframe, population or denominator when available, and an uncertainty note when any of those are absent.
