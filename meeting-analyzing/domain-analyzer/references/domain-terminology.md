# Domain terminology and normalization

Preserve official capitalization and distinguish portfolio, product, feature, and persona names. Cross-check spellings against dated official sources supplied for the item.

The profile's `terminology.brands` list provides the common brand and product names for the configured domain. The profile's `terminology.naming_rules` define domain-specific conventions.

Do not assume a current branding relationship or product integration from name proximity alone. Record the source material's exact wording and the date of the official cross-check.

## Availability vocabulary

Use only the terms configured in the profile's `availability_vocabulary`. Standard terms include:

- `available-now`
- `generally-available`
- `announced-no-date`
- `coming-in-weeks`
- `later-this-year`
- `demo-only`
- `uncertain`

When sources differ, retain both dated statements and explain the discrepancy.
